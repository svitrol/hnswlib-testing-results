7. Why hnswlib misses the nearest neighbour

For each query, t is the true nearest neighbour and r the best returned point. The level-0 graph is read from the saved index and searched with BFS. A miss gets the first matching reason: unreachable (t cannot be reached from the entry point), early_exit (the run without early exit found t), hub_route (r and t both within 2 hops of a well-connected node), adjacent (r to t within 2 hops), low_in_degree (t has very few in-links), far (none of these). Well-connected nodes: the top 1 % by in-degree, nodes with a full neighbour list, and nodes on upper levels.
